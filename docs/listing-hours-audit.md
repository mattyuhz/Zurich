## Maintenance rule

- Prefer the operator, venue, or station landlord over generic directories.
- Bind every schedule to the exact branch named by the listing.
- Do not translate a trail or neighborhood into a misleading business-style “open” state.
- Recheck regular schedules and seasonal boundaries when the guide review date changes.
