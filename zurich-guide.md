# 0. How to Use This Document

## Status labels

- 🔴 **MUST** — actively seek this out
- 🟠 **HIGH** — excellent; prioritize when convenient
- 🟡 **GOOD** — worthwhile, especially when nearby
- ⚪ **OPTIONAL** — legitimate option but don't plan around it
- 🔵 **REFERENCE** — useful context that probably doesn't need prominent website placement
- 🟣 **RESEARCH** — promising, but needs more investigation/verification
- ⬇️ **DEPRIORITIZED** — recommendation intentionally not being pursued
- ❌ **EXCLUDE** — don't publish to the primary guide unless circumstances change

## Core principle

The objective is **not to complete Zürich**.

Prioritize experiences that answer at least one of these:

1. Is this distinctly Zürich?
2. Is this distinctly Swiss and especially good here?
3. Is Zürich unexpectedly excellent at this?
4. Is this unusually aligned with personal interests?
5. Is it excellent enough to justify using limited travel time?
6. Does it fit naturally into an area already being visited?

"Good restaurant in Zürich" is not enough.

### Food philosophy

Food is a major way of understanding a city.

Useful analogies:

- NYC → pizza + hot dogs
- Chicago → deep dish/tavern pizza + Chicago dogs
- Southern California → tacos
- Bay Area → burritos
- San Gabriel Valley → exceptional Chinese food that outsiders may not expect

For Zürich, seek both:

**A. Foods Zürich is known for**

and

**B. Foods/cuisines Zürich is unexpectedly excellent at.**

Do not equate "best X in Zürich" with "worth eating while visiting Zürich."

## 🔴 Zürcher Geschnetzeltes + Rösti

**Status:** MUST / PUBLISH

### What

Thinly sliced veal in a creamy sauce traditionally served with rösti.

### Why care

This is one of the clearest answers to:

> "What food actually belongs to Zürich?"

It is much more relevant to the trip than simply eating generic "Swiss food."

### Context

The dish is strongly associated with Zürich and is commonly treated as one of
the city's defining culinary specialties.

### Order

**Zürcher Geschnetzeltes with rösti**

Prefer a traditional preparation for the first experience.

### Candidate restaurants

#### Zunfthaus zur Waag
**Priority:** HIGH

- Historic guild-house setting
- Traditional Zürich experience
- Strong candidate for destination version
- Better fit for a deliberate Zürich meal than simply ordering the dish wherever

#### zum Kropf
**Priority:** HIGH / GOOD

- Traditional setting
- Historic atmosphere
- Candidate alternative

#### Zeughauskeller
**Priority:** OPTIONAL
- Famous
- Extremely central
- Historic arsenal setting
- High-volume / tourist-heavy
- Mixed signal from Googler recommendations

### Website note

Show **one primary restaurant + one backup**, not every place serving the dish.

## 🔴 Wiedikerli

**Status:** MUST / PUBLISH

### What

A Zürich sausage associated specifically with Wiedikon.

### Why care

This may be one of the best matches for the desired "NYC hot dog" style of travel-food experience:

- inexpensive
- local
- casual
- neighborhood-specific
- culturally recognizable
- not a formal tourist meal

### Context

Created by the Keller butcher family in Wiedikon in the 1990s.

The name comes from **Wiedikon**, making this a surprisingly modern local food tradition rather than an ancient Swiss recipe.

### Order

**Original Wiedikerli**

### Area

Wiedikon / Kreis 3

### Pair with

- neighborhood wandering
- Collective Bakery
- coffee
- Gertrudhof later

## 🔴 Sternen Grill

**Status:** MUST / PUBLISH

### What

One of Zürich's iconic sausage institutions.

### Order

**St. Galler bratwurst + Gold Bürli + Sternen mustard**

### Important

The mustard is famously strong.

### Why care

This appeared repeatedly and independently in the Googler recommendations.

It isn't the same thing as Wiedikerli:

**Wiedikerli**
→ hyperlocal Zürich sausage

**Sternen Grill**
→ iconic Zürich sausage-eating institution

Both can justify a stop.

### Strategy

Use the casual downstairs/street-level experience.

Do **not** unnecessarily convert this into a formal restaurant dinner.

### Area

Bellevue / near lake / Old Town edge

### Best use

- lunch
- snack
- opportunistic stop during Old Town walk

## 🟠 Birchermüesli

**Status:** HIGH / PUBLISH

### What

Muesli based around oats, fruit, nuts and dairy.

### Why care

Invented in Zürich by physician **Maximilian Bircher-Benner** around the turn of
the 20th century.

Originally connected to his nutritional ideas rather than being invented as a
hotel breakfast buffet food.

### Strategy

Do not dedicate a major meal to it.

Best used as:

- breakfast
- light breakfast
- snack

### Research

- [ ] Identify one particularly good Zürich version
- [ ] Determine whether any historically meaningful place still serves a noteworthy version

## 🟠 Luxemburgerli

**Status:** HIGH / PUBLISH

### Where

**Confiserie Sprüngli — Paradeplatz**

### What

Small, delicate macarons associated with Sprüngli.

### Why care

Despite the Luxembourg-sounding name, they became a Zürich specialty through Sprüngli.

### Strategy

Buy a **small mixed assortment**.

Eat fresh.

No need to turn this into a major dessert expedition.

### Pair with

Old Town / Bahnhofstrasse / Paradeplatz wandering.

## 🔴 Museum für Gestaltung Zürich

**Status:** MUST / PUBLISH

### Why

Switzerland's major museum devoted to design and visual communication.

Relevant areas include:

- graphic design
- posters
- typography
- industrial design
- visual communication
- historical and contemporary design

# 24. Practical Zürich

## Transit

### Primary tool

**SBB Mobile**

Use for:

- trains
- trams
- buses
- connections
- tickets
- platform information

### EasyRide

Potentially easiest low-stress solution for ordinary transit.

General workflow:

1. Open SBB Mobile.
2. Start EasyRide before boarding.
3. Travel.
4. Stop EasyRide after completing journey.
5. Appropriate fare is calculated.

### Important

Swiss transit generally does **not** use American-style turnstiles everywhere.

A lack of gate does not mean transit is free.

Ticket inspections occur.
